Frequently Asked Questions
When registering your business or renewing a package (from UGX 3,000+), select MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money at payment checkout. Enter your phone number to receive an instant USSD prompt on your phone to confirm your PIN.
Buyers can view your listed products and tap the "Contact via WhatsApp" or "Call Seller" button directly on your business page. Orders can be discussed and finalized straight to your phone.
Each package tier (e.g., Starter, Silver, Gold) allows a specified number of active item listings. If you hit your limit, you can upgrade your plan anytime under Vendor Packages in your Profile tab to unlock more slots.
Venx connects sellers directly with local buyers across Uganda. Delivery terms (boda boda dispatch, pick-up point, or courier) are coordinated directly between you and the buyer/seller during checkout or WhatsApp chat.
Go to your Profile tab, tap Edit Profile Details, update your shop name, primary district/address (e.g., Kampala Plaza), or phone number, and click Save.
